Squishy fit check

For developers, not linked from the store. Draws every squishy, and a mystery egg with sunglasses, at full squash, full stretch, and rest, every 6° of turn, at the size of the store's 3D view in each layout from phone to 12.9-inch iPad. It reports how close each comes to the edge of the view, as a share of the view's height. Every number must be above 0; if one isn't, make that squishy's size in items.js smaller. Takes a few minutes.
